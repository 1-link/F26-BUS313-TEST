Course Project Instructions

The course project counts for 20% of your final grade and integrates course concepts with current economic dynamics. AI tools (Google Gemini) are used to augment research, learning, and prototype creation under a "human in the loop" workflow. Cite all AI tool usage.

Course Project: Part One

Write a brief report that includes charts, images, and valid sources based on the following instructions:

  • Product Description: Choose one product type from: Candy bar, Motorcycle, Cosmetic product, or Shoe or Boot.
  • Industry Trends: Include a summary of industry and economic trends impacting your chosen product's market.
  • Prototype Advertisement: Create a product image advertisement submitted in .png, .jpg, or .gif format.
  • Price & Cost Breakdown: Provide average retail price and cost breakdown (industry averages acceptable).
  • Supply & Demand: Create a hypothetical supply and demand curve with both numerical values and a visual curve.
    •
  • Breakeven Point: Calculate and state your product's breakeven point.
  • Sourcing & Manufacturing: Summarize where raw materials will be sourced and where the finished product will be manufactured.
    •
  • Market Scope: State whether you plan to sell domestically, abroad, or both.
  • Subsidies: Identify any subsidies that may assist production.

Course Project: Part Two

Write a brief report assuming sales in both domestic and foreign markets:

  • World Price: Find the average world price of your product.
  • 10% Tariff Analysis: Chart the impact of a 10% tariff on product price and demand. Explain who wins and loses (consumers, producers, government).
    •
  • 5% Subsidy Analysis: Chart the impact of a 5% subsidy and explain the impact on consumers, producers, and government.
  • Market Reactions: Explain how consumers and producers are likely to react under each scenario.
  • Net Gain/Loss Calculations: Calculate net gain/loss for producers, consumers, and government tax revenue from the 10% tariff.
  • Retaliation Assessment: Briefly explain if foreign competitor governments are likely to retaliate with tariffs or subsidies.

Policies

Attendance and Participation

Attendance and course participation factor into your final grade. Active participation in class discussions and case studies is required. Points will be deducted for lack of participation and more than two unexcused absences.

Exam & Make-up Policy

You must have a written medical emergency to be absent from an exam. If allowed, make-up exams are scheduled at the discretion of the instructor, and the instructor reserves the right to deduct points. Make-up exams may be more rigorous. Exam regrade errors must be addressed with the instructor within a week after results are posted.

File Submission Guidelines

All materials must be submitted through Laulima. Files must be named as follows: LastNameInitialAssignmentName (e.g., VillarrealPA1.xlsx). Do not use any spaces, dashes, commas, or punctuation in file names.

Academic Integrity & CBA Conduct

All relevant CBA policies regarding academic honesty, grievance procedure, and confidentiality apply. Cheating, plagiarism, unauthorized assistance, or using work/exams from previous semesters or online will result in failing the class.

Classroom Professionalism

Spamming the class, attempting to speak on behalf of other students, or engaging in disrespectful/disruptive behavior will result in failing the course. Professional verbal and written communication is required.

Course Content Variation & Withdrawal

Exact dates and details are subject to change and will be announced through Laulima. Refer to the Office of Student Affairs for the last day for in-person restricted withdrawal ("W" grade).
